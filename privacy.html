Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

What Markom Drive accesses

Markom Drive signs in to one Google account used by the team. It only works with the Google Drive folder the team sets aside for it and with files people deliberately send it as a link. It reads, creates, and moves files inside that folder, and reads linked files. It does not use Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google data.

How the data is used

Files are copied to a private server controlled by the team's operator, processed there for the team's own work, and results are written back to the same folder. Content may be processed by AI services the operator uses under its own accounts.

Markom Drive does not sell data, does not share it for advertising, and does not give it to anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping access

The team can stop access at any time by removing the folder share or by revoking Markom Drive in the Google account's security settings. The access token is then deleted from the server.
